# Study card portraits: credits

The Genius Study card image (`img/study-faces-by-chart.jpg`) shows people who are plotted on the people chart, both axes numeric. From the left, they follow that chart: closest to the top-right corner first, then the rest of the strip in the same order. Source commit 15afac2 (26 Oct 2026) of [neuresthetics_genius_study](https://github.com/neuresthetics/neuresthetics_genius_study). This is where those people sit on the chart, not a ranking of genius. Every file is public domain or under a free license, as listed below. The strip as a whole is shared under CC BY-SA 4.0 because it includes CC BY-SA portraits.
