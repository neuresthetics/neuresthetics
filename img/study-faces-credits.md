# Study card portraits: credits

The Neuresthetics Genius Study card image (`img/short-study-faces.jpg`) is a collage of 285 portraits of people on the study's roster (core status), taken from Wikimedia Commons. Every file is public domain or under a free license, as listed below. Each portrait was cropped to the face, toned sepia and resized; the far crowd repeats these portraits (some mirrored) and adds simple silhouettes, for 1,380 figures in total, the size of the study's roster (`data/roster/roster.csv` in [neuresthetics_genius_study](https://github.com/neuresthetics/neuresthetics_genius_study)).

Licenses: CC BY and CC BY-SA files are used under those licenses with the attribution given here; the collage as a whole is shared under CC BY-SA 4.0. Larger faces are ordered by 2025 English Wikipedia page views, which is not a ranking used by the study.
