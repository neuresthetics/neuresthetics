## Background: Spinoza, *Ethica*, Part I, 1677

- **Source:** [File:Spinoza Ethica Pars1 Prop1.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Spinoza_Ethica_Pars1_Prop1.jpg) on Wikimedia Commons ([original file](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/0/07/Spinoza_Ethica_Pars1_Prop1.jpg)).
- **What it is:** pages 2–3 of *Ethica ordine geometrico demonstrata*, Part I (*De Deo*), from Spinoza's *Opera posthuma* (Amsterdam, 1677). The Commons description reads "Spinoza's Ethica Ordine Geometrico Demonstrata, Part 1, axioms and propositions I-IV."
- **Crop used:** the left page, the *Axiomata* heading and Axioms I–II ("Omnia, quæ sunt, vel in se, vel in alio sunt."), with the ink extracted and drawn as faint sepia on near-black.
- **License:** public domain (published 1677). Commons credit line: http://www1.odn.ne.jp/gakuju/kyokai/spkLocator-e.html

## Foreground: the book's dependency graph

Drawn from the front matter of [`candidates/core_v2/`](https://github.com/neuresthetics/freedom_of_necessity/tree/main/candidates/core_v2) in [neuresthetics/freedom_of_necessity](https://github.com/neuresthetics/freedom_of_necessity) (MIT License). It has 12 entries and 33 links:

- 6 support links, from each entry's `cites:` list (drawn bright and solid);
- 25 definition-use links, from each entry's `terms:` list (drawn thin);
- 2 "explains" links (PR1 to A3 and A4), from the citation graph in `candidates/core_v2/README.md` (drawn dotted).

Columns follow the longest chain of dependencies, from left to right. Axioms are the brighter nodes, and A1, the root, is the brightest. Definitions are hollow circles, and the principle PR1 is a diamond.
